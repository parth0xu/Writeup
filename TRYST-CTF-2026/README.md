## Event Overview

TRYST is IIT Delhi's flagship annual technical festival. The CTF event was held as part of TRYST'26 with a **₹40,000 prize pool**, drawing participants from colleges across India competing in a live, in-person format.

- **Format:** Jeopardy-style, in-person
- **Venue:** IIT Delhi, New Delhi
- **Duration:** Multi-day (Feb 27 – Mar 1, 2026)
- **Prize Pool:** ₹40,000
